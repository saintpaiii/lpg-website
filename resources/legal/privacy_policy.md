{company_name} (the "Platform", "we") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, why, who we share it with, and your rights under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

## 1. Information we collect from customers
- Name, email address, phone number, home address, city and barangay.
- Saved delivery addresses and map pins. Your device location is used only when you press "Use my current location".
- Valid ID and selfie photo, if you complete identity verification.
- Orders, payment status, coupons used, loyalty points, ratings, reviews, refund requests, reports and chat messages.
- We do not store card or e-wallet details. Online payments are handled by PayMongo.

## 2. Information we collect from sellers, staff and riders
- Name, email address, phone number and store details.
- Business documents: valid ID, BIR registration, Business Permit, FSIC, DOE LPG license and LTO permit.
- For riders: location while using the rider map or making a delivery, and proof-of-delivery photos.
- For staff and riders: the location of the device when clocking in and out, and attendance and payroll records entered by the store.

## 3. Information we collect from everyone
- Login records (date, time, IP address and device/browser) for security.
- If you sign in with Google: your name and email address from your Google account.
- When you agree to our Terms or this Privacy Policy: the version, date, IP address and browser, as proof of your agreement.

## 4. Why we use your information
- To create and secure your account and verify your identity.
- To process, deliver and track orders, compute delivery fees and find stores near you.
- To process payments, refunds, commission invoices and coupons.
- To send notifications and emails about your account and orders.
- To handle reports, disputes and safety issues.
- To prevent fraud and keep the Platform safe.
- To create summary reports (without naming you) that help us improve the service.

## 5. Who we share it with
- The store you order from and its rider: your name, phone number, delivery address and pin, so they can deliver your order.
- Customers see a rider's approximate location (about 1 km) while their order is on the way. The store sees its rider's exact location.
- PayMongo — to process online payments.
- Service providers that run the Platform: email delivery, real-time updates (Pusher), web hosting, and map services (OpenStreetMap-based search and routing, which receive the address text or coordinates you search).
- Government authorities when required by law.
- We do not sell your personal information.

## 6. How long we keep it
- While your account is active.
- Order, payment and invoice records are kept as long as required by Philippine tax and accounting rules.
- For riders, only the latest position and the most recent points of each delivery are kept.
- You can ask us to close your account by emailing {company_email}. We deactivate it and delete or anonymize the data we do not need to keep by law or to settle a dispute.

## 7. How we protect it
- Passwords are stored hashed (never in plain text). Access to personal data in the Platform is limited by user role and permissions.
- Uploaded IDs and documents are shown in the Platform only to the account owner and authorized Platform staff.
- Connections to the Platform use HTTPS.

## 8. Your rights
Under the Data Privacy Act you have the right to:
- be informed about how your data is used;
- access your personal data;
- correct wrong or outdated data (you can edit most of it in your Profile);
- object to processing or withdraw consent;
- ask us to delete or block your data;
- get a copy of your data (data portability);
- file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).

## 9. Children
The Platform is not for persons below 18 years old. We do not knowingly collect their data.

## 10. Changes to this Privacy Policy
If we make important changes, you will be asked to read and accept the new version the next time you open the Platform.
